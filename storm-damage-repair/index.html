Warning Signs for Storm Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Sagging or detached gutters that spill rainwater down the siding
  • Water getting past doors, windows and wall penetrations when wind pushes the rain
  • Water pooling in the attic, around the chimney or near skylights
  • Water stains that show up on ceilings or walls during or after a storm
  • Ceiling drywall that sags or bulges, or paint that bubbles after rain
  • Metal flashing pulled away from vents, valleys or the edge of the roof
  • Rooms that start to smell stale or moldy following a storm

Storm Damage Repair Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • For insurance purposes, keep a yearly set of pictures showing the state of your property
  • Ice dams form when attics run warm, so check that yours has enough insulation and airflow
  • Schedule a roof inspection every year so weak spots are repaired before storm season
  • Extend downspouts and slope the soil so runoff moves away from the foundation
  • Check window and door seals each year and replace any caulking that has cracked
  • Clean out downspouts and gutters regularly, which lets heavy rainfall drain away as it should
