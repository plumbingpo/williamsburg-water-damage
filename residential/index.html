Water Removal

Water Removal for Your Home

Storm flooding, appliance failures, and burst pipes all put water where it should not be, and having a trained crew remove it is the quickest, safest fix. From a small leak to a fully flooded floor, Williamsburg Restoration removes water from homes across Williamsburg. You get five things with every visit: an inspection of what got wet, heavy-duty extraction, a full moisture map, protection for your contents, and paperwork your insurance claim can use.

Standing water poses a serious risk to your family, your home, and your belongings. It takes only hours for drywall to swell, hardwood to warp, and electrical components to start corroding. If left untreated for 24 to 48 hours, mold growth becomes almost inevitable. Even a small puddle that should not be there is reason enough to call a professional right away. The sooner we arrive, the sooner we can measure what is wet and get extraction going, ahead of any secondary damage.

Not every job starts with a flood, and some come from a concealed leak that has run for days or even weeks. A slow leak behind a wall or under a floor can cause extensive structural damage before you notice any visible signs. To track down trapped moisture and verify everything is dry, our crew relies on thermal cameras and professional-grade meters.

Complete Dry-Out

Drying Your Home's Structure Properly

Complete structural drying is the most critical step in preventing long-term damage to your home after water intrusion. Proper drying eliminates hidden moisture from walls, subfloors, and cavities, prevents mold growth, and restores your home's structural integrity. We take and log moisture readings daily, and each Williamsburg Restoration drying job is run according to IICRC S500.

Our residential drying setup covers high-velocity air movers, commercial dehumidifiers, specialty systems made for wall cavities and hardwood floors, plus thermal imaging to keep verifying moisture as we go. No two houses dry the same way, so we build the plan around your layout, your building materials, and how bad the damage is.

Professional drying goes much further than most people expect. Even after visible water is gone, moisture trapped in wall cavities, under flooring, and in structural framing can fuel mold growth and cause wood rot for weeks. We monitor moisture levels daily and don't consider the job complete until every reading reaches pre-loss standards. Depending on how much got wet, drying a home usually takes 3 to 5 days.

Mold Prevention

Home Mold Remediation and Prevention

Mold can begin growing within 24 to 48 hours of water exposure, spreading rapidly through your home and creating serious health risks for your family. Williamsburg Restoration provides professional mold prevention as part of every water damage restoration project, as well as standalone mold remediation for existing growth. For mold work, our IICRC-certified team sticks to the EPA guidance and the IICRC S520 standard so the job is both safe and complete.

Any time a home has water damage, our advice is to include a mold prevention treatment. Once mold has settled in, removing it is harder and more expensive than using an antimicrobial treatment during drying. However, if you already have visible mold or suspect hidden mold from a previous water event, we provide comprehensive testing, containment, removal, and post-remediation verification to ensure your home is safe and healthy.

Sewage Cleanup

Safe Sewage Cleanup in Your Home

Few kinds of water damage are as dangerous to a household as a sewage backup. Category 3 black water contains bacteria, viruses, and other pathogens that pose serious health risks. To clean up sewage safely, Williamsburg Restoration sends technicians with the right training and gear who work under strict IICRC protocols on every contaminated water job.

The work runs in six steps: sealing off the area, safely pumping out the sewage, hauling away materials beyond saving, antimicrobial cleaning, full structural drying, and a final test confirming the house is safe. One team does the whole job so that your family is back in a clean and healthy house without delay.
