DIY vs Professional Water Damage Restoration in Williamsburg, VA

Home / Blog / DIY vs Professional Water Damage Restoration in Williamsburg, VA

When water damage strikes your Williamsburg, VA home, one of the first decisions is whether to handle the cleanup yourself or call a professional restoration company. Most water losses turn out to be more involved than they look, even though handling them yourself seems like the cheaper route. Read on to learn where the line sits between a do-it-yourself job and one that calls for professional restoration.

When DIY Cleanup May Be Appropriate

Very small, contained water events may be manageable without professional help. Clean water, such as a sink overflow, that touched only non-porous hard floors, stayed below roughly 10 square feet, and was removed within the hour is usually fine to deal with yourself. Similarly, a small spill on tile or vinyl that is mopped up immediately, with the area dried by fans and a dehumidifier, probably does not require professional help. Four things have to be true: clean water, a small area, immediate cleanup, and porous materials that were not significantly affected.

Situations That Need a Professional

Most of the water damage people actually face is beyond a do-it-yourself fix. Do not wait to call if any of these apply: a musty odor that points to early mold, a source you are unable to locate or stop, possible water in wall cavities or framing, contaminated water (black or gray), wet drywall, carpet, or similar porous materials, several hours of standing water, or a wet area bigger than 10 square feet.

The Hidden Costs of DIY Water Damage Cleanup

In Williamsburg, a do-it-yourself cleanup often costs the homeowner more in the end than hiring a restoration company would have. The first reason is mold, which grows within days when drying is incomplete and adds $1,500 to $6,000 or more for remediation. Wet materials that are dried incorrectly keep breaking down, so more has to be replaced. The cost of rented extraction and drying gear, fans and dehumidifiers included, mounts quickly while performing below a professional setup. If the cleanup is poorly documented, your insurance payout may be smaller. Finally, every day lost to a cleanup that is not working pushes real restoration back and lets the damage grow.

What a Professional Crew Brings

Professional water damage restoration provides several advantages that DIY cannot match: truck-mounted extraction that removes water hundreds of times faster, commercial drying equipment that actually dries wall cavities and structural materials, thermal imaging that finds hidden moisture, monitoring that verifies complete drying, antimicrobial treatment that helps prevent mold, thorough documentation that supports your insurance claim, and the peace of mind of knowing the job is done right.
