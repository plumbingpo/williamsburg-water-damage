Roof Leak Water Damage Help in Williamsburg

Where you see the stain and where the rain gets in are usually two different places. A loose shingle, a failed flashing joint or an aging seal around a skylight lets rain in, and it travels across the roof deck and framing until it falls into the insulation. A ceiling stain tends to arrive late, well after the space overhead first got wet. We find the full wet area and dry it out.

To be clear, roof repair is not what we do, water damage is. Inside the home, our team takes out saturated insulation, dries the wood and drywall, treats any mold and repairs the ceiling. If rain is still coming in, we can tarp the roof as a temporary cover, or work alongside the roofer you choose so the leak is fixed before we close anything up.

First Five Minutes

Your First Steps

  1. Shut Off Wet CircuitsIf water is dripping through a light fixture, a ceiling fan or near an outlet, switch off that circuit at the breaker and leave the fixture alone.
  2. Contain the WaterFurniture, electronics and rugs should be moved aside, with buckets placed wherever water is falling. Lay towels or plastic sheeting down to protect the floor.
  3. Relieve a Ceiling BulgeA ceiling that sags with trapped water can be drained: stay off to the side and make a small opening at its lowest spot, with a bucket underneath.
  4. Stay Off the RoofClimbing on slick shingles or crawling around a dark attic is not worth the risk. From indoors, mark where the water enters, and let professionals handle everything else.
  5. Photograph the DamageBefore anything is moved or discarded, get video and photos of the attic, the ceiling and damaged items, and then phone us so drying can begin.
Common Causes

Why This Happens

  • Missing or broken shingles, often after high wind, with bare underlayment beneath.
  • Gaps at chimneys, vents and sidewalls where the flashing has separated or rusted through.
  • Leaves and grit collecting where two roof slopes meet, damming rain until it seeps through.
  • A skylight whose curb or seal has dried and cracked, so rain gets past it.
  • Popped or exposed roofing nails, each one a tiny opening for rain.
If It Is Left

Where the Damage Spreads

Drywall Ceilings

Drywall that stays wet stains, sags and loses its strength. Joints open, tape peels, and a waterlogged piece may fall without much notice.

Lights and Wiring

Recessed cans, ceiling fans and electrical boxes are low points where roof water collects and drips through. Keep that circuit off until someone has inspected it, since damp wiring may corrode or short out.

Wall Cavities

Rain sometimes follows the framing into a wall, where the insulation and the hidden side of the drywall take it up. Bubbling paint or a swollen baseboard may be the only sign.

Mold in the Attic

Damp framing, paper-faced drywall, warmth and darkness give mold what it needs. Growth can begin within 24 to 48 hours and spread across the underside of the decking.

How We Fix It

What Happens After You Call

Insurance

What About Insurance?

Interior damage from a sudden, accidental roof leak, like one caused by a fallen limb or wind-torn shingles, is often covered under a homeowners policy. Insurers generally turn down claims tied to wear, age or lack of upkeep, and they may handle the roof repair on different terms than the inside damage. Our team gives your adjuster photos and moisture readings, yet what is covered comes down to your policy, so check it or talk to your agent.

Cost

What Drives the Price

  • Finish work, including matching an existing texture and painting the room.
  • Damage beyond the ceiling, such as wet floors, wall cavities or furniture.
  • How much attic insulation is wet and whether it has to be removed.
  • How easy the attic is to reach, how high the ceilings are, and if the roof needs a tarp before work begins.
